The current viewer

The viewer in use today. It is published separately from this site and is under active development.

It brings the Ruthwell, Bewcastle and Brussels crosses together in a single edition: each cross has a 3D model you can turn and light for yourself, set beside chapters on its scanning campaign, its history and its iconography, with a glossary and a table of contents for the whole edition. The models run in 3DHOP, the open source heritage viewer built by the same ISTI CNR group that scanned the crosses, which streams the multiresolution meshes a piece at a time so a full-resolution monument will load in an ordinary browser.
