Outputs & Collaborations

HDI is conducted in partnership with the Humanities Innovation Lab and the Lethbridge Journal Incubator at the University of Lethbridge, and through collaborations with researchers, cultural institutions, infrastructure organizations, and community partners internationally. Research data is made openly available through established repositories.

Project outputs include

  • Research publications and comparative case studies
  • Digital humanities and cultural heritage projects
  • Infrastructure, governance, and policy frameworks
  • Best-practice and data ethics resources
  • Workshops, presentations, and training materials
  • Open-access and community-oriented digital scholarship initiatives

The work is meant to serve humanities researchers, the information professionals who design research infrastructure, and the institutions, students, and publics who rely on humanities scholarship.
