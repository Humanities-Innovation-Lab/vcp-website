New in the viewer

All Three Crosses, One Edition

The viewer was rebuilt over 2026 and now holds the Ruthwell, Bewcastle and Brussels crosses together. Each has a 3D model you can turn and light for yourself, set beside chapters on how it was scanned, and, for Ruthwell, on its history and iconography. A glossary and a table of contents cover the whole edition, and work on it is ongoing.
