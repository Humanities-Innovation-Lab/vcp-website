Funding

The Visionary Cross Project has been funded in whole or in part by two major grants from the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council (SSHRC), with additional support from the Canada Foundation for Innovation and the University of Lethbridge.

  • CA$62,430 2010–2014

    Crossroads: Editing the Visionary Cross Matrix in Anglo-Saxon England

    SSHRC Standard Research Grant · 410-2010-1474

    Funding for a scanning campaign of the Ruthwell Cross.

  • CA$9,640 2014–2015

    DigiCultH: Engaging with Digital Cultural Heritage Objects

    GRAND Startup Grant · G-CI-14-LB-01

    Funding to support continued work on the project.

  • CA$233,224 2015–2019

    What Goes Around: Editing the Anglo-Saxon Visionary Cross Cultural Matrix

    SSHRC Insight Grant · 435-2015-1119

    Funding for the scanning campaign of the Bewcastle Cross.

  • CA$86,937 2017–2019

    What Goes Around: The Visionary Cross Digital Library

    CFI John R. Evans Leadership Fund · JELF 32819

    Funding to continue carrying out project needs.

  • CA$6,000 2024

    Stone to Screen: The Visionary Cross Digital Edition

    Chinook Summer Research Award

    Funding granted to student researcher.
